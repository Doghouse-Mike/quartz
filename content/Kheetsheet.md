# Kheetsheet

For installation instructions, and to peruse the code, see [the repo](https://github.com/Doghouse-Mike/kheetsheet). For you Arch/hyprland folken, I present [this](https://github.com/Doghouse-Mike/kheetsheet-hypr/)

Kheetsheet is very much an homage to Cheatsheet. It was a neat little bit of software that did one thing well.

The TLDR on it is that it sits there silently, hiding on your computer, not doing anything until you're struggling to remember a keyboard shortcut. Then just hold down a keyboard shortcut(`CTRL+/` by default) for a couple of seconds, and a nice, friendly overlay shows on screen, telling you all the currently available keyboard shortcuts, what their shortcuts _are_, and letting you click on the one you want to enact it.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/Kheetsheet-Kheetsheet-1.gif)

So you get access to the tools you didn't know, but may have suspected, were at your disposal all along, and potentially begin to ingest/memorise what those shortcuts are. Theoretically this means less time doing "computer" and more time doing the actual _work_, even if that work is to play games.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/Kheetsheet-Kheetsheet-2.png)
