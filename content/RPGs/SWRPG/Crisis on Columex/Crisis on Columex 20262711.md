# Crisis on Columex 20262711

Nira got the [Force-Sensitive Outcast](https://doghouse-mike.github.io/swrpg-wiki/careers--and--specializations/force-sensitive-outcast) tree and worked down it a bit. It's gonna be spicy even before we get that lightsaber working.

Docs to heal people, with Snowy chipping in

Deebo to scout - 2 boosts to planning roll

Ed and Snowy to anticipate their moves based on gangs n warfare

Nira to try and reattach Kemmie's arm, then set up some traps (boomers) with his help

Then Ed to set up some blinds to fire from
