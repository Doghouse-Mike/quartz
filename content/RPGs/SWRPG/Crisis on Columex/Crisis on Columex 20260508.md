# Crisis on Columex 20260508

## Or Securing the Cure

Something about that title had a ring to it.

Aaaand Action!

Three guys had rappelled in, and started taking up positions. They were outfitted like stormtroopers, but all in black.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/20260625T134701359Z.png)

Hostile One spotted our KX droid first and blasted his poor little arm off. Sad sad sad.

Hostile Two went for [[Mouth]]. Mouth took a dive, avoiding damage and getting himself in to cover.

[[K3-M1]] took a swing at the trooper that had shot him. It was absorbed by the armour, but sent him skittering over the floor to fall at [[Snowlakka|Snowy's]] hairy feet. This little manoeuvre also put the droid on the floor though, as his software adjusted to the revised weight distribution.

Snowy smacked the prone guy (H1) with her axe. Those anatomy lessons paid off and he took a good blow to the head, damaging the helmet, but was still ticking.

Mouth popped out of cover, [guns blazing](https://star-wars-rpg-ffg.fandom.com/wiki/Guns_Blazing_talent), unleashing a flurry of bolts at Hostile Two. Some of the shots went wild, but he peppered the trooper with fire. One of his blasters overheated momentarily. Someone had been slacking on their maintenance during the downtime.

The third trooper entered the fray. Seeing Mouth as the biggest threat, opened fire on him. They mostly went pinging off his armour, though he was feeling the heat.

"Fire in the hole" [[Nira Keth|Nira]] screamed, chucking a proton grenade at Hostile Two. Melted em a bit, but he was still standing.

[[Z-X3ED|Ed]] popped a couple into the chest of Hostile Two. He didn't seem phased by the attack, beckoned em on, calling for more. He was gonna reap the actions of those consequences.

Hostile One skittered across the floor, back towards the stairs, taking shots at Snowy as he did so. Snowy tried to dodge it, but took one to the armpit. Bellowing in rage.

Everyone was picking on Snowy, the second trooper shot at her too! Caused a bit more damage.

Nira fired off a frag grenade this time, Hostile Two went down, the heat and concussion scrambling whatever was inside that armour. His buddies that were bunched around him (rookie error, being that close) took a bit of heat from the blast, shrapnel slamming into them. The blast wave cleared some of the smoke they'd unleashed too.

All the action appeared to have awoken something in the sectioned off room (to the south). There was a bit of a commotion/banging coming from there now, we heard faint groans.

Mouth opened up on Hostile Three again. Began shooting wild, [took a minute to collect himself](https://star-wars-rpg-ffg.fandom.com/wiki/Natural_Commando_talent), and melted stuff, punching some holes in that armour.

```
((13 damage twice! There was a Triumph *and* a Despair in there too.))
```

Then realised some of the shots from his initial flurry had hit K3, pushing him [towards the brink](https://sw-eote-srd.vercel.app/personal/status-recovery/critical-injuries#:~:text=At%20the%20Brink:%20The%20target%20suffers%201%20Strain%20each%20time%20they%20perform%20an%20action.).

Snowy spiked themselves with a stimpack while Hostile Three shot at Mouth again, but hit nothing but air.

Ed shouted at Pinky (why this was what he'd decided to call K3 remains to be decided) to get up and do something, "Blast em!" and shot at Hostile Three, catching em right in the neck joint.

Pinky pulled himself up the wall, pulling a blaster out and levelling it at H3. Shot him right in the back, the blast putting him down on his face, apparently out of commission. Pinky took a minute to dust himself off, muttering "Like that?" under his breath. Or what would be his breath, if he breathed.

That was two down, one to go!

Hostile Two pulled something out of his pocket, dived across the room and whacked a [data spike](https://star-wars-rpg-ffg.fandom.com/wiki/Dataspike) of some kind into one of the computers, which started uploading something. The lighting flickered, and the door to the lab zone creaked open. There were groans (and not from the door).

The idiot had released the infected!

Nira handed Snowy their fusion cutter (to better crack through the armour) TKTKTK witty one liner.

Snowy fired it up and got the saboteur. The cutter sank through the armour, and eviscerated the bugger. Anatomy lessons came in again, and guts came out. The guy made a lot of noise, then shut up suddenly, permanently.

The infected were shambling towards us, in what remained of their uniforms. Dr Voss was cowering in a corner. There's no cure for death. If there was anything she wanted to say to them she should do it now.

Ed blasted the nearest group, one went down. Shouted "Aim for the head, shoot em in the meat and they just keep going".

Pinky spotted the contaminants, and started blasting.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/20260625T134048381Z.jpeg)

Took out the rest of that group. "Yes, you shoot most things in the head, they tend to die"

Mouth got some shots off, opening up with both guns, literally mowed through one of the other groups of shamblers.

Shooting them enough time also appeared to kill them (again).

They were shambling at Snowy, I guess her brain smelled best? Because she was the smartest?

The axe came out again, and Snowy made a mess of em, cleaving through their rotting meat. Two were still ticking though.

Ed took careful aim and popped another in the cranium.

Nira ran over to the last one standing, primed a grenade, shoved it in his mouth, and gave em a good kick out of the way.

Big badaboom.

Aaaand breathe! Well, some of us.

Snowy applied some first aid.

Nira patched Kemi up a bit. Still missing an arm, but not leaking any more, at least.

Ed wanted to salvage whatever we wanted from here and get out before anyone else turned up.

Time to go full loot goblin!
