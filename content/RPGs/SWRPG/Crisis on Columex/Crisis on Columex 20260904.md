# Crisis on Columex 20260904

## [[Crisis on Columex 20260814|Previously, on....]]

Looted some corpses, we had _a lot_ to carry. And some new toys.

Nuked the lab, their backups (apart from a couple we were carrying), and any means of producing more of the virus.

There were some fights with turrets, and a sneaky deathtrooper.

We hadn't taken any samples of the virus, but did have the vaccine, and the method to produce more, if not the means (hello hospital peeps).

Mouth got blown up pretty good. He was walking wounded, but _walking_. Turned out he was a clone trooper too. The surviving staff from the lab (Dr Guy and whatsherface), and the big droid were coming with.

## Credits Roll, Aaaand Action!

Got Mouth patched up and ready to move, fuelled by painkillers and stimulants.

Dust and smoke all in the air. Emergency lights were still on, but it was pretty dark in here. The door was an absolute ruin.

Coerced the civilians into keeping Mouth upright for the trip to the hospital.

Ed checked for any remaining traps, and to see if there was anyone waiting outside. Traps were clear, and there didn't seem to be anyone out there. The sun was going down though, so we'd be travelling through dusk, potentially into the night.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/RPGs/SWRPG/Crisis%20on%20Columex/Crisis%20on%20Columex%2020260904/20260904T221908386Z.png)

The troopers conveniently left their ride outside though, amateurs! They were playing incognito, so it was an unmarked civilian speeder, convertible, rather than anything military.

Nira sidled up to it to try and get it running. Got the engine going, the repulsors fired up. It was a bit sputtery, but functional.

Everyone began to pile in, it would be a squeeze, but would fit. These troopers were fans of country music, from what came blaring out of the speakers.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/RPGs/SWRPG/Crisis%20on%20Columex/Crisis%20on%20Columex%2020260904/Crisis-on-Columex-20260904-Credits-Roll-Aaaand-Action-3.png)

Ed spotted a group of 4 walking towards us, recognised them as being part of Nero's crew. He was trying to hurry everyone along, to no effect.

They asked us what was in there, if we were ok, and whether that explosion and cloud of smoke were anything to do with us. We played it off as taking out an Imperial facility, simply looting it for some blasters and gear, but that Mouth was badly injured.

They offered to take some of the guns off our hands. We hadn't got time for shenanigans, so only traded one of many new grenades for a repair patch instead.

They said the boss (Neero) was moving the attack up to tonight, so we needed to hurry up and infiltrate the hospital.

"You boys stay safe now, street lights are on" Ed said as they sent em off.

Ed was driving, the others were struggling to see in the dark. Luckily for us, the streets were pretty clear, and it was an uneventful drive.

## The Hospital

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/RPGs/SWRPG/Crisis%20on%20Columex/Crisis%20on%20Columex%2020260904/Crisis-on-Columex-20260904-The-Hospital-1.png)

We called ahead to let them know we were inbound with wounded. Aware that comms weren't secure, we didn't say much else, and tried to keep it subtle. No mention of a vaccine or anything else that we'd been up to. Dumped the car to fill that gap in the South-East corner of the barricade.

Safely negotiated around the traps protecting the entrance, remembering from last time.

Back through the testing room, everyone got screened. The gaps in Mouth's armour had let the germs in though, he'd got the lurgy.

Fortunately, Snowy and Illara got lucky, and despite the damage to their PPE, remained uninfected. Illara being in the clear reinforced that the vaccine had worked!

Dr Guy tested positive though, he was off to quarantine. "If you want to test this vaccine on someone, that one volunteers".

Dr Jackie, (shalom) started asking how it was going, then saw the state Mouth was in and got him rushed to a bacta tank/med droid. "Get him in the dip!" called Jackie, ordering some flunkies around to get him looked at.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/RPGs/SWRPG/Crisis%20on%20Columex/Crisis%20on%20Columex%2020260904/20260904T221910378Z-1.png)

Moving back to her office, Snowy explained our adventure, going into a lot of detail about the lab, utterly burying the lede on the fact that **WE HAD A VACCINE**. Ed flashed his coat open, showing racks of vaccine vials. Jackie lost her absolute mind. Conveyed the details of how it worked, testing done so far, etc.

Working backwards, Snowy had skipped right over our encounter with Neero on the way _to_ the lab.

Explained the encounter with him and his goons, and their plan to use us to infiltrate the hospital, disable their defences, and attack.

Jackie was _pissed_ that the attack was coming tonight. Neero had been a patsy for the empire all along, and trying to limit the effectiveness of the hospital. Referred to him as a "Son of a Hutt", which puzzled Ed, as he'd classified Neero as a Rodian. Decided to run a thorough diagnostic at the next opportunity, or chalk it up to "you all look the same to me".

There was a bit of a panic going on. The hospital had limited defenses, and (healthy) people to man them, there was no way to move this many people in time, especially as night was approaching.

We're going to try turning Neero's plan back on him by having the turrets powered down, everything looking quiet, then luring them into a killbox.

Jackie had plans to form, and a lot of other work to do, but needed our help in defending the place. Ed did offer to help get the message out instead, but Routh was out of the bacta, and would cover that from the hololink upstairs.
