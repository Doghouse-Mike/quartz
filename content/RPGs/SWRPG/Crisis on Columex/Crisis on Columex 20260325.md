# Crisis on Columex 20260325

Back in the saddle!

Recap from [[RPGs/SWRPG/Crisis on Columex/Crisis on Columex 20251119]]

Virus, blah blah, _always_ fatal. But appeared to be engineered.

"Nero" and his crew were poaching the (limited) supplies n stuff that came through.

Concerns that the Empire will use the outbreak as an excuse to torch the sector.

Reminder that the virus was reanimating the dead. They mostly came out at night, mostly.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/20260625T220810808Z.jpg)

Moxley was here! The guy we busted for cheating at cards.

The doc's name was Jackie Goodman.

'Ganics got some sleep, Ed pottered around, skipped to the morning, everyone was well rested.

Doc directed us to the canteen for refreshments. Topped up on caff, prepped for the expedition, checked the map. Had a KM or two to cover. The plan was to roll stealthy and observant.

["Sneaking, we're sneaking"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=36PrcE6Xn5Q) Ed said, earning a dirty look from Nira as they blended into the shadows.

"Did you hear that" we heard as Snowy and Ed blundered out into a street.

Three guys, one in a forklift (or sporklift), two assisting. Not in imperial uniforms, but were all rocking the same blasters (of imperial origin).

\~~"Just taking my dog for a walk" said Ed.~~

Told em we were out after medical supplies, they offered to show us their wares. Disclosed that anything that came in/out went through them.

We decided to pass on through. They weren't having it though, had to pay the toll. Snowy tried telling them about our actual mission. They asked if we were from the hospital. So nooow we were off to see their boss instead. Turned out there was a bunch more of them in the warehouse behind, so it might have been good that we didn't start blasting. There was almost a fight over who got to say "I have a bad feeling about this".

To the hotel! It would have once been lavish, but time had taken its toll. Too many fluids spilled and grime accumulated.

There was a bar.

Tools/weapons/supplies etc laying around. Snowy tripped over a crate, Nira stubbed a toe, got pointed and laughed at.

Ed clocked some _clearly_ Imperial/military crates.

We were waiting.

A Rodian came out. Fat Greedo. This was Neero.

Speaking in Huttese, said he had a "particular set of friends" that had an interest in the hospital, and the "terrorists" that were holding the patients "hostage". We needed to talk our way out of here, stat.

Wanted us to Trojan Horse em into the hospital. We agreed because otherwise we'd be killed, but pointed out we couldn't show up empty handed. Called on some lackeys who gave us a few vials of something, a datapad, and a portable drive that'd nerf the hospital defenses once we were in. And a comm link.

Off we headed. Walking casual. Ducked into a nearby building to check we weren't carrying a bomb or something. The stuff was mostly harmless. Malware looked like it'd do what it said. Code rang a bell with Nira, reminded her of the guy that was involved in her dad's death. So there was a tie there. Not the ship.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doghouse-Mike/quartzpics/main/assets/20260625T220812946Z.jpg)

Carried on our merry way towards the lab.

Looked like we'd found ground zero! Was a warehouse. No people about, and nothing particularly outstanding, ~20 meters high. Kinda like an ikea from the outside, without the colour. Beige, with some windows higher up, otherwise kinda boring. Like those "definitely not an FBI van" jokes.

Clocked the office/visitors entrance, and the loading bays. Nira spotted an entrance to the cellar tucked in a blind spot behind the building. It looked more high tech than the surroundings.

So we headed for that. Obvs a trap.

Tried inserting our ISB code cylinder. Great success! Some beeps and the door popped open.

Headed into a corridor that led to a bit of a lobby. Some signs on the wall, nothing clear, "black wing" "operations" and "lab".

"Doors and corners, people" Ed said as they headed towards the lab. The lab vs ops decision was made by a roll of Ed's lucky chance cube.

Ominous music and a fade to black as the crew headed towards the lab, 15XP richer
